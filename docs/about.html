SELECTED EVIDENCE

What the projects show

The EEG studies use the public PhysioNet EEGMMIDB dataset and report results for a declared participant-wise evaluation protocol. In the graph-learning project, EEGNet reached 70.1% mean participant accuracy; the distance-graph GCN did not significantly outperform its identity-graph control after correction.

The Wilson–Cowan model and closed-loop project are software simulations. Their outputs help inspect model behavior, but are not biological measurements, EEG decoding results or clinical evidence.
