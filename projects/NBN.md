# Neuro-Behavior Network (NBN)

## Long-term R&D concept

NBN (Neuro-Behavior Network) is a long-term research concept for computational modelling of human state from multimodal physiological and behavioural signals.

### Core idea

Explore how AI/ML models can represent changing human-state patterns from multiple streams of biological and behavioural data, while accounting for individual baselines and uncertainty.

### Research themes

- Multimodal physiological signals
- Neural and behavioural data
- Human-state modelling
- Temporal machine learning
- Personal baselines and individual variability
- Uncertainty-aware decision support
- Human-centred AI

### Relationship to NEURO STORE

NEURO STORE is a concrete wearable sensing prototype. NBN is the broader modelling/R&D direction that could eventually use multimodal data from wearable systems and other sensing platforms.

### Research status

Conceptual R&D project. Specific datasets, model architectures and experimental claims should be added only after they are actually implemented and evaluated.
