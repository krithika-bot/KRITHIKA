# NEURO STORE

## Research prototype

NEURO STORE is a non-invasive wearable research prototype for longitudinal monitoring of neuro-muscular activity and movement.

### Research question

How can wearable physiological and motion signals be collected and analysed over time to characterise an individual's movement and muscle-activity patterns?

### Planned sensing stack

- EMG for muscle activity
- IMU for movement and motion features
- ESP32-class embedded platform
- Bluetooth Low Energy for data transfer
- Mobile application for data collection / visualisation
- Personal-baseline analytics

### Research direction

The project is intended as a learning and research platform rather than a diagnostic or treatment device. Future work includes signal-quality assessment, preprocessing, feature extraction, longitudinal analysis and multimodal modelling.

### Why it connects to neuroscience

The project provides a practical bridge from AI/ML into physiological signal analysis: the computational problem is not only classification, but understanding noisy biological signals, individual variability and temporal patterns.

### Current status

Research/prototype stage. Hardware, acquisition and analysis components are being developed and documented incrementally.
