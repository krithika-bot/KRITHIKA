# Award Cycle Source Check — NSA 6.0

**Checked:** 18 November 2026  
**Target pathway:** National Startup Awards 6.0, Student Innovator / Aspiring Entrepreneur special category.  
**Application status:** not submitted.

The official Startup India page states the 2026 student route is open to eligible school and undergraduate/college students. Its 18–22 age group is called **Emerging Entrepreneurs**. The official student application form says a registered startup, finished product, working prototype, or commercialized solution is not mandatory; ideation-stage submissions are accepted. It asks for a pitch deck (preferably 5–10 slides) and supporting images/documents where available, and says the application is English-only. The published application window is 27 October to **20 November 2026**.

## Submission check before pressing Submit

1. Reopen the official form and verify the deadline, category, age band, student status, required fields, size limits, and declaration on the day of submission.
2. Use the applicant’s real name, institution, contact information, and contribution description. Do not submit a claim or number that is not supported by the evidence.
3. Treat the current software tests as software behavior evidence only. Physical sensor, human, field, clinical, and soldier-benefit evidence remains unavailable.
4. Do not submit the project as “successfully deployed,” “diagnostic,” “whole-body monitoring,” “first,” or “life-saving.”
5. The application declaration says information must be true and correct; the applicant must personally review and submit it.
