PROVISIONAL DESIGN ENVELOPE

Keep the wearable light; measure before claiming

Starting engineering target only: complete wearable electronics, battery, enclosure, straps, and cables together should aim for ≤150 g. Chest hub enclosure target: ≤75 × 55 × 25 mm. These are not measured specifications; exact boards and battery are not selected.

The pack-load fixture is a separate bench experiment. It adds hardware and does not make a soldier’s carried pack lighter.

Bench setup can compare known loads and record distribution. Dynamic walking, comfort, load reduction, and cold-weather reliability remain untested.

Designed around sensor limits

The idea began as a single-point full-body monitor. That is not technically credible. This concept shows separate sensor locations and explicitly limits what each can say.

  • Wrist optical data may fail with movement, poor contact, or cold.
  • Chest motion can describe movement, not memory loss or mental health.
  • Surface temperature is not core temperature or a diagnosis.
  • Alerts should say “re-check / seek qualified support,” never “you are safe.”
