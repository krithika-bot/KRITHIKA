PROVISIONAL DESIGN ENVELOPE

Added mass is part of the test

Initial design goal only: complete removable sensor kit target ≤150 g, including force links, electronics, battery, enclosure, cables, and mounts. This is unmeasured and may need revision after parts are selected. Final mass will be weighed, not estimated as a result.

The monitor does not make the carried pack lighter. The prototype tests load-share measurement and fit-check cues only.

Keep the two research questions separate

This body map is illustrative and does not represent a built prototype. The current hardware-first R&D question is relative pack load-share measurement; physiological monitoring is not used to justify its novelty.

  • A single point cannot monitor the whole body.
  • Wrist optical readings can be affected by motion, contact, and cold.
  • Surface temperature is not core temperature or a diagnosis.
  • No alert should claim “you are safe.”

HARDWARE-FIRST NOVELTY HYPOTHESIS

Cold- and motion-aware load-share estimate with an uncertainty gate

Existing patents already describe shoulder/hip load-balance sensing and alerts. Candidate contribution: a removable, offline retrofit that reports relative load share only when sensor checks pass, and withholds prompts under drift, motion corruption, or detachment.
