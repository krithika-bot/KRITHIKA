# Research Portfolio

This directory contains research-oriented documentation for projects at different stages of development.

## Research-stage labels

- **Concept** — research direction or hypothesis that has not yet been experimentally validated.
- **Prototype** — an early implementation or hardware/software proof of concept.
- **Experiment** — a study with a defined protocol and collected data.
- **Manuscript Draft** — a research manuscript prepared for discussion; not necessarily peer reviewed or published.

## Current projects

- [NEURO STORE](../projects/NEURO_STORE.md) — wearable EMG/IMU research prototype.
- [NBN](../projects/NBN.md) — Neuro-Behavior Network conceptual R&D framework.

The portfolio deliberately separates demonstrated work from proposed research and does not present unpublished results as established findings.
